Career: interview preparation

Recruiter screening questions

Prepared Oct 21, 2026. Questions a recruiter might ask to judge fit for Cloud Infrastructure Engineer and AI Engineer roles, drawn from the requirements in the two job-lead lists. Recruiters are usually not engineers, so most questions probe for specifics they can check: scale, tools, outcomes, and what the candidate personally did.

Both roles: screening

  1. How many years have you worked in this area full-time, and how much of that was production work rather than labs or side projects?
  2. Are you looking for full-time, contract, or either? Remote, hybrid, or on-site?
  3. What compensation range are you targeting?
  4. Do you need visa sponsorship now or in the future?
  5. Walk me through your most recent role: what did you own, and who relied on your work?
  6. Tell me about a project you're proud of. What was your part, and what was the team's?
  7. Can you share anything I can look at, such as GitHub, a portfolio, a live demo, or a write-up?
Listening for: Answers that are concrete and consistent, and clearly separate what the candidate did from what the team did.

Cloud Infrastructure Engineer

From the requirements in this lead list.

Infrastructure as Code

  1. What have you built with Terraform (or CloudFormation or CDK)? About how many resources or accounts did it manage?
  2. How did you organize your Terraform: modules, state storage, separate environments?
  3. Have you ever had to fix infrastructure that was changed by hand outside the code? How did you handle it?

Kubernetes and containers

  1. Have you run Kubernetes in production? Was it managed (EKS, AKS, GKE) or self-hosted, and about how many services ran on it?
  2. How did applications get deployed to the cluster: Helm, Argo CD, something else?

CI/CD

  1. Describe a pipeline you designed. What stages did it have, and what did it improve?

AWS architecture and security

  1. Which AWS services have you used hands-on, not just read about?
  2. Have you set up a multi-account AWS environment? How did you control access across accounts?
  3. Which security services have you configured, such as GuardDuty, Security Hub, Config or CloudTrail? What did you do with the findings?
  4. How have you connected cloud networks to on-premises networks: VPN, Direct Connect, Transit Gateway?

Networking and identity

  1. Have you set up single sign-on into a cloud environment from a corporate identity provider like Entra ID or Okta?
  2. Tell me about a DNS or routing problem you had to troubleshoot.

Observability and operations

  1. What monitoring stack have you run, and what did you alert on?
  2. Tell me about a production incident you worked on: what broke, what you did, and what changed afterward.
  3. Have you been on an on-call rotation?

Linux and scripting

  1. How many Linux servers have you managed, and which distributions?
  2. What's something you automated with Python or Bash that saved real time?

Compliance and government

  1. Do you hold a security clearance (Public Trust, Secret, TS/SCI), or can you obtain one?
  2. Have you worked under FedRAMP, NIST 800-53, STIGs or CIS Benchmarks?
  3. Which certifications do you hold, and which are in progress? When do you expect to finish them?
Listening for: Numbers (accounts, servers, services), real incidents they lived through, and whether "I built" actually means "I maintained what someone else built."

AI Engineer

From the requirements in this lead list.

Python and backend

  1. What's your main language? What have you built in it that's running in production?
  2. Have you built services that stream LLM output to users? How did you handle latency and errors?

LLM applications and agents

  1. Describe an agent or LLM feature you built from start to finish. What problem did it solve, and who used it?
  2. Which frameworks or SDKs have you used (LangGraph, Anthropic SDK, OpenAI Agents SDK)? Why did you choose them?
  3. How did you make the model's output reliable enough for other systems to use, for example structured output or schema validation?
  4. Have you built a RAG system? What made the answers better or worse?

Evaluation and quality

  1. How did you know your AI system was working? What did you measure?
  2. Have you built an evaluation suite? What was in it, and how did it affect what shipped?
  3. How did you catch it when a prompt or model change made things worse?
  4. How have you traced or monitored LLM calls in production for cost, latency and failures?

Cloud and infrastructure for AI

  1. How was your AI system deployed: which cloud, containers or not, infrastructure as code or not?
  2. Have you worked with model gateways or managed AI services like Bedrock, Azure OpenAI or Vertex AI?

AI coding tools

  1. How do you use Claude Code, Codex or Cursor day to day?
  2. Tell me about a time an AI coding tool gave you something wrong or misleading. How did you catch it? (Central for the G2i evaluation roles.)

Data

  1. Have you built data pipelines that feed AI systems? What volume, and what tools?

Client-facing and leadership

  1. Have you worked directly with clients or business stakeholders to scope and deliver an AI project?
  2. Have you mentored engineers or led design reviews?
Listening for: Real users and real results rather than demos, evaluations rather than gut feel, and an honest account of what didn't work.

Answers that raise red flags

Apply to both roles.

  • Only tool names, no outcomes ("I used Terraform, Kubernetes, LangChain")
  • No numbers at all for scale or impact
  • "We" for everything and never "I"
  • Every project is a tutorial, course lab or unfinished demo
  • Can't describe a failure or incident
  • Certifications listed as "in progress" with no expected completion date
