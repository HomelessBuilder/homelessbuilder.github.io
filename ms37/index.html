Meta-System 3.7

Two pages to read before a test install. One explains how the planned onboarding interview is meant to work. The other explains how the system itself works once it is running.

Meta-System is a working environment built around Claude Code: several AI sessions with defined roles, a shared record of decisions and ideas, and a browser app that puts all of it on one screen. Version 3.7 is the line meant to work for someone other than the person who built it.

Both pages say what is ready and what isn't. Some pieces are finished and in daily use. Others are still drafts. The pages mark which is which.

  1. Page oneThe onboarding interviewThe first-run conversation that is meant to set up a new install, what it builds, and where it stands today. It is a draft and is not connected to 3.7 yet.
  2. Page twoUser guideHow the system works: panes, sessions, personas, workspaces, restarting after a reboot, the decision log and idea bank, and the rules every session follows. Includes what an install consists of today.
