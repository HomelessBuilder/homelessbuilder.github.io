The Whole Account

A dramatization of the Bible in composition order, from the oldest recoverable Israelite poetry through Revelation — ten parts plus one interlude, each scene built on documented historical-critical scholarship rather than invention, with genuine uncertainty kept on the page rather than resolved for the sake of a cleaner story.

Canon scope: the full Protestant sixty-six, the Catholic/Orthodox deuterocanon, and material unique to the Ethiopian Orthodox tradition — 1 Enoch, Jubilees, and Meqabyan — read as scripture by the communities that kept them.

Reading order: composition order, not canonical order. Paul's letters come before any Gospel, because they were written first. The Song of Deborah opens the whole account, centuries before the Pentateuch reached the form most readers know.

  1. Part OneThe Song Before KingsFive archaic poems: the muster and Jael's tent, the sea, Balaam's oracles, and two deathbed blessings.
  2. Part TwoThe House and the ThroneThe Ark's capture and return, David's household coming apart from the inside, and the earliest named prophets.
  3. Part ThreeWhat the Prophets CarriedIsaiah's three voices, Jeremiah's burned scroll, Ezekiel's sign-acts, Lamentations, and the exile's end.
  4. Part FourWhat Was SalvagedPriestly reconstruction, Nehemiah's wall, Ezra's mixed-marriage crisis, and the wisdom tradition's own argument.
  5. Part FiveWhat Ethiopia KeptThe Watchers, the horn on the ram, the throne in the Parables, Jubilees, and the book that has yet to be asked about.
  6. Part SixThe Same War, Told Four WaysTobit, Ben Sira's grandson, the Maccabean crisis in two keys, Judith's hand, and Alexandria's answer.
  7. Part SevenLetters Before Any GospelSeven letters written a generation before any Gospel existed, from a grieving church to a prisoner's request.
  8. Part EightA Generation LaterFour accounts of one life, written a generation apart, growing bolder about who this man was as they go.
  9. Part NineA Movement Learning to WaitThirteen later letters organizing a movement for a future longer than anyone first planned for.
  10. Part TenThe Closing VisionDaniel's court tales and visions, then Revelation's seven letters, seals, and a tree offered to every nation.
Interlude
  1. InterludeThe Hand That Wove Them TogetherHow the Song of Deborah, the Ark Narrative, and the Court History were woven into one continuous history, twice. Sits chronologically between Part Two and Part Three.
